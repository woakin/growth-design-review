# UI Rules of Thumb

Context-specific heuristics for different page and flow archetypes.

## Landing Pages
- **Rule 1 (Singular Focus)**: Must have *one* primary conversion goal above the fold. Secondary CTAs should be ghost/outline or demoted.
- **Rule 2 (Credibility)**: Use real human faces, product UI screenshots, or interactive demos instead of generic vector illustrations.
- **Rule 3 (Early Social Proof)**: Display social proof (customer logos, star ratings, user counts) before the primary scroll threshold.

## E-commerce & Product Pages (PDP)
- **Rule 1 (Sticky Buy Action)**: The primary Add-to-Cart (ATC) button and current price must remain accessible (sticky bottom bar on mobile scroll).
- **Rule 2 (Friction Reducers near CTA)**: Display shipping terms, delivery estimates, return policy, and payment security badges immediately adjacent to the Buy button.
- **Rule 3 (Visual Clarity)**: Product gallery must support zoom/swipe, show customer review averages above the fold, and provide immediate visual feedback on variant selection (size, color).

## Cart & Checkout Flows
- **Rule 1 (Distraction-Free Environment)**: Strip standard navigation headers, footers, and exit links during checkout. Keep users focused entirely on completion.
- **Rule 2 (Express Payment Acceleration)**: Position 1-click payment methods (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, PayPal) at the top of checkout to eliminate manual form entry.
- **Rule 3 (Effortless Forms)**: Use inline address autocomplete, smart defaults, automatic card type detection, and clear error messages directly next to affected fields.

## Pricing & Plan Tables
- **Rule 1 (Visual Anchor)**: Designate one tier as "Recommended" or "Most Popular" with distinct visual elevation (border, badge, scale).
- **Rule 2 (Annual Savings Callout)**: Default to annual billing with transparent, explicit savings callouts (e.g., "Get 2 months free" or "Save 20%").
- **Rule 3 (Outcome-Oriented Features)**: Group features by user benefit/outcome rather than technical specs; keep rows scannable with clear checkmarks and tooltips.

## Onboarding Flows
- **Rule 1 (Time-to-Value - TTV)**: Minimize the number of steps required to reach the user's first "Aha! moment" (ideally under 2 minutes).
- **Rule 2 (Gradual Commitment)**: Defer mandatory registration, passwords, or payment info until *after* the user has experienced initial core value.
- **Rule 3 (Progress Transparency)**: Provide numbered steps or progress bars with meaningful milestone markers to leverage momentum.

## Dashboards & Web Apps
- **Rule 1 (North Star Hierarchy)**: Position the top 1–3 "North Star" metrics at the very top of the dashboard.
- **Rule 2 (Zero Chart Junk)**: Eliminate redundant grid lines, heavy 3D charts, and unnecessary border noise. Let data breathe.
- **Rule 3 (Actionable Anomalies)**: Visually distinguish anomalies, alerts, or pending actions with distinct status tags and direct action links.

## Mobile-First Heuristics
- **Rule 1 (Thumb Zone Ergonomics)**: Primary actions and navigation triggers must sit within the natural thumb zone (lower 40% of the screen).
- **Rule 2 (Generous Touch Targets)**: Interactive elements must meet the minimum 44x44px touch boundary with adequate spacing to prevent accidental taps.
- **Rule 3 (Bottom Sheet Drawers)**: Use bottom-anchored modal sheets for filters, options, and actions instead of centered desktop dialog boxes.
