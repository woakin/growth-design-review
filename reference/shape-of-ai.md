# 🔷 Shape of AI Framework

When presented with an AI-Native interface (e.g., LLMs, generative UI, autonomous agents), apply the **Shape of AI** framework to diagnose UX friction and propose specific AI patterns to solve them.

## The 5 Categories of AI Patterns

### 1. Wayfinders (Onboarding & Prompting)
Use these to solve "Blank Canvas" paralysis and help users understand what the AI can do.
- **Madlibs:** Provide structured templates where users fill in specific blanks.
- **Auto-fill:** Predict and populate user inputs based on context.
- **Suggestions:** Offer pre-written prompts or actions to guide the user.
- **Inline Actions:** Embed AI triggers directly within the context of the user's workflow.

### 2. Tuners (Refinement)
Use these when users struggle to control or refine the AI's output.
- **Filters:** Allow users to narrow down AI generations using specific criteria.
- **Modes:** Provide distinct settings (e.g., Creative vs. Precise) to alter the AI's behavior.
- **Parameters:** Expose dials, sliders, or input fields to adjust specific aspects of the output.
- **Attachments:** Empower users to ground the AI by uploading relevant context (files, images).

### 3. Governors (Human-in-the-Loop)
Use these to mitigate cognitive overload or lack of agency during autonomous tasks.
- **Action Plans:** Present a step-by-step breakdown of what the agent intends to do before it executes.
- **Stream of Thought:** Surface intermediate tool calls or logical steps while the AI is processing.
- **Verification:** Require explicit user approval before the AI takes high-stakes actions.
- **Branches:** Allow users to explore multiple paths or alternate outcomes simultaneously.

### 4. Trust Builders
Use these to solve friction related to accuracy, bias, or data privacy.
- **Citations:** Link directly to the sources the AI used to generate its response.
- **Disclosure:** Clearly label when content is AI-generated and explain its limitations.
- **Footprints:** Leave a transparent trail of how the AI reached its conclusion.
- **Watermarks:** Visually or cryptographically mark generated media as synthetic.

### 5. Identifiers
Use these for brand consistency and anthropomorphization.
- **Avatar:** Assign a visual representation to the AI.
- **Iconography:** Use consistent symbols or metaphors to designate AI-powered features.
- **Personality:** Define a distinct tone of voice and behavioral traits for the AI.
